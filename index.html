How it works

1

Explore the map

Find walks filmed on real streets and open one from a pin or the list.

2

Take the walk

Hold the Move stick to walk forward, and drag to look around. On a phone, that is the whole control set.

3

Share your walk

Upload a clip you filmed. In this preview the file never leaves your device, and the form is not saved.
