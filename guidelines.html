Community guidelines

This page is a draft outline, not a finished policy.

  • Share walks you filmed yourself.
  • Do not include copyrighted music you do not have rights to.
  • Be careful with recognizable faces and voices. Ask for consent, or avoid showing people clearly.
  • Do not film private spaces or anything unsafe or illegal.
  • A real launch would review new walks before they appear on the map.
