Controls and limitations

Push up to walk along the recorded path. Sideways turns the view. Drag the picture to look around. Release to stop.

Pull back once to jump five seconds earlier. Release before another jump. This is a jump between recorded points, not continuous backward walking. At the start of a route, there is no earlier point.
