Sample 360 footage (stand-in — not map footage, not creator footage)
====================================================================

File: sample-360.mp4 (and sample-360-poster.jpg)

This is a short, downscaled, muted excerpt of:

  “Anıtkabir ( Atatürk ) 360 Derece Video Panorama Gezinti - 360 Degree Video.webm”
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:An%C4%B1tkabir_(%20Atat%C3%BCrk%20)_360_Derece_Video_Panorama_Gezinti_-_360_Degree_Video.webm
  Author: 360TR
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
  License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Original: 3840×2048 equirectangular walking 360 video, recorded at Anıtkabir, Ankara.
  This excerpt: about 22 seconds starting ~00:20, scaled to 1280×682, H.264, no audio,
  used as a labeled stand-in for the creator-trial preview.

The app never presents this clip as footage from a map pin or as a creator upload.
The same credit is shown whenever the clip plays.

Attribution to show in the app:

  Anıtkabir, Ankara — 360TR, CC BY 3.0.

Lower-resolution first-load encode
==================================

File: sample-360-low.mp4

A smaller, faststart (moov-at-front) H.264 encode of the same source clip,
used only to show a first frame faster. The player then upgrades to the
full-resolution file. Same author and license (360TR, CC BY 3.0).

(including Kyoto, Street intersection, Ludlow, and the Grove/Farmers test
