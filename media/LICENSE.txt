Files no longer in this tree
============================

The unlicensed YouTube test clips previously stored as hollywood-360.mp4
and hollywood-360-2d-converted.mp4 (and their posters) were removed from
the project going forward. They must not be served. Git history may still
contain them; they are not part of the current tree.

Lower-resolution first-load encodes
===================================

Files: sample-360-low.mp4 and walk-of-fame-flat-low.mp4

These are smaller, faststart (moov-at-front) H.264 encodes of the same
source clips above, used only to show a first frame faster. The player
then upgrades to the full-resolution file. Same authors and licenses
as the corresponding full files (360TR, CC BY 3.0; Dimitri Baret,
Pexels License).

Placeholder avatar (simple figure)
=================================

  No third-party character file. The walker is built in
  src/splat-player.js with three.js (MIT): a glowing, rounded 3D men's
  restroom pictogram figure (egg-shaped head, natural neck, medium build,
  hip-length arms, two-segment legs and small feet) with a procedural
  walk/idle cycle, composited in a separate MSAA pass after the splat
  with a soft screen-space glow rather than a per-part outline.
  Used as a stylized stand-in in the splat room, not as a real person at
  the map pin.
