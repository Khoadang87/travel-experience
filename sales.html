Put your business on the Walk of Fame Globe

People explore Hollywood Boulevard on our globe, zoom to the street map, and tap pins to walk the sidewalk in 360°. A pin puts your business on that walk — with your name and a link to your site or booking page.

Demo of the offer: this page shows what a business pin would cost and include. No business has bought a pin yet, and the prices below are working figures to review — not validated sales. Nothing on this page charges you or signs you up.

What a buyer gets

  • A 60-second clip pin of your location — your own 360 footage, or flat footage shown on a curved screen in the 360 player.
  • Live on the globe for 1 year, with your business name, a short description, and a link to your site or booking page.
  • Steerable viewing: visitors look around with the joystick and walk the clip forward and back at a slow stroll.
  • See a walk in action: open the map and tap Start at The Grove to view the Los Angeles walks currently on this preview.

Request a free preview

The request goes through the submission form on this site. This prototype is a mock: nothing is uploaded to a server, emailed, or charged — the form records your request in this browser only, and the preview uses a labeled 360 stand-in (Anıtkabir, Ankara — 360TR, CC BY 3.0), not your footage and not Hollywood footage, until a real preview is made for you.

How it works

  1. Request a free preview — send your location and footage through the submission form. We make a 45-second watermarked preview of your spot.
  2. Review your preview — open it on the map, walk it with the joystick, and decide if it shows your business the way you want.
  3. Pay only if you want the full pin — Standard $250 or Featured $500 (figure to review), live for 1 year. If you don’t want it, the preview simply expires.
