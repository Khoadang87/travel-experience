About

This is a draft preview of an official travel site. The idea is simple: walk real streets, filmed by real travelers, then share a walk you filmed yourself.

What you can try now: explore three Los Angeles walks (The Grove, Farmers Market, and a café counter on 1st Street), use the player, and walk through the sign-up and upload screens.

What this preview does not do: create real accounts, upload or store video, take payment, or publish a walk to the live map.

The site name is an interim placeholder until the official name is decided.

Older “For businesses” and “Creator submission” pages from an earlier preview now point here and to Share your walk.
